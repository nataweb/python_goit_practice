This task focuses on analyzing the latest Stack Overflow Developer Survey dataset using Pandas in Jupyter Notebook or Google Colab. The aim is to practice working with real-world survey data by applying techniques of data cleaning, filtering, grouping, and aggregation. During the analysis, you will determine how many respondents participated in the survey and how many answered all questions, calculate measures of central tendency such as the mean, median, mode, and quantiles for developer experience, and explore work arrangements, including the number of people working remotely. You will also measure the percentage of respondents who program in Python, identify how developers learned to code with a focus on online courses, and analyze the compensation of Python developers across different countries by comparing mean and median yearly salaries. Additionally, the task includes identifying the education levels of the top five highest-paid respondents. As a bonus, you may also investigate the percentage of Python programmers across different age groups and explore the most common industries among highly compensated remote developers.
